# Product brief: Northstar thermos

Northstar is a reusable stainless-steel travel thermos for commuters. It keeps
drinks hot for 12 hours and cold for 24 hours, has a leak-resistant lid, and is
available in slate blue and cream. The launch price is 32 USD. The brand voice
is practical, calm, and specific. Avoid hype, invented testimonials, and claims
about health or environmental impact that are not in this brief.
