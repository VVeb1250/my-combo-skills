# Research

Frame the decision or question, resolve definitions and distinguish observed facts from inference. Prefer primary, current evidence where material; compare applicability and contradictory findings. Do not turn factual uncertainty into a user interview.

- [Research and decisions](../combo/methods/research.md): source evaluation, comparisons, business analysis and scoped synthesis.
- [Experiment](../combo/methods/experiment.md): empirical claims needing comparable observations.
- [Techniques](../combo/methods/expertise.md): specialized evidence or version-sensitive facts.

Stop at the requested deliverable. For durable project knowledge, synthesize consequential findings in its canonical location only when writes are authorized.
