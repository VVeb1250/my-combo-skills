# Engineering

Ground the desired behavior in accepted contracts and real callers. Choose the smallest complete intervention; do not preserve a disproven model. Establish independent expectations before production edits.

- [Align](../combo/methods/alignment.md): consequential user choices, ambiguous domain terms or conflicting intent.
- [Design](../combo/methods/design.md): responsibility, state, interfaces, reuse or structural changes.
- [Behavioral implementation](../combo/methods/implementation.md): test seams, vertical slices, regression and equivalence.
- [Experiment](../combo/methods/experiment.md): uncertain mechanism or expensive-to-reverse choices.
- [Techniques](../combo/methods/expertise.md): unresolved platform behavior or hidden domain risks.
- [Assurance](../combo/methods/assurance.md): actual outcome and affected callers.
- [Wiki](../combo/methods/wiki.md) and [Git](../combo/methods/git.md): unfamiliar documentation ownership, safe checkpoints or isolated changes.

Reconcile changed knowledge and checkpoint meaningful progress during implementation, not as a separate final task. For a tiny understood edit, use the existing seam and focused check.
