# Assurance

Identify the requested acceptance target and revision. Read the connected code or actual artifact and its contract; determine what evidence could falsify success.

- [Assurance](../combo/methods/assurance.md): risk-based review, behavioral, visual and integrated verification.
- [Techniques](../combo/methods/expertise.md): specialized evidence when ordinary checks miss the risk.
- [Implementation](../combo/methods/implementation.md): evaluate independent test oracles and seam coverage.
- [Wiki](../combo/methods/wiki.md): check known drift and authority conflicts.
- [Git](../combo/methods/git.md): compare patch series, affected scope and recoverability.

Report concrete failures, consequences, evidence and unverified gaps. Do not change files on review-only or verify-only requests.
