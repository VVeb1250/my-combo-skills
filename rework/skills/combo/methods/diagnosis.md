# Diagnose causes

Establish a specific observed symptom, relevant environment and expected behavior. Redact secrets from commands and evidence. Build the tightest feedback loop that discriminates the symptom: existing test, direct CLI/API, actual browser journey, captured trace, seeded replay, property probe or targeted instrumentation. For flaky behavior measure reproduction rate; pin input/time and isolate state where possible. If the environment cannot reproduce it, identify the missing evidence and limit conclusions; do not invent a cause.

Trace the failing real call chain to ownership and violated invariant. Rank plausible causes by what would change the next decision. Make an observation whose result differs between hypotheses, then discard unsupported ones. If multiple attempted fixes share a premise, test that premise directly; do not require failed attempts before checking an already supported structural contradiction. Use targeted history or [bisect](git.md) for a historical regression with a usable oracle; first bad revision is a lead, not proof of mechanism.

Correct a consequential false mental model in the [wiki](wiki.md) as soon as established, within scope. Select [repair/refactor/rework](design.md) by cause. For authorized fixes, guard against recurrence at the real seam or structural invariant and rerun the original symptom and affected adjacent behavior. Stop at diagnosis for read-only requests. If an intervention fails, preserve evidence, recheck the premise and the observation method before trying another patch.
