# Wiki

Use [wiki](../combo/methods/wiki.md). Inspect existing canonical documents first, preserve accepted requirements, resolve contradictions against evidence and expose decision-relevant relationships. Do not migrate or delete legacy records without authority. For audit-only, report rather than edit. Normal wiki maintenance is already a continuous responsibility, not this invocation.
